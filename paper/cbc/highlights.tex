\begin{itemize}
\item Benchmarks compare PLN and GLMNet on 20 real microbiome datasets.
\item Held-out LOTO-CV identifies when latent count models improve prediction.
\item Sample-to-taxon ratio is the strongest predictor of model performance.
\item PLNNetwork is strongest for broad undirected microbial interaction graphs.
\item GLMNet is competitive when samples are abundant relative to taxa.
\end{itemize}
